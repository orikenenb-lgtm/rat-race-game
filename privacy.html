Privacy Policy — Rat Race (מירוץ העכברים)

by Kinan Studio · Last updated: 27 November 2026

What we collect

  • A nickname you choose. No real name is required.
  • A random player ID created by the game.
  • Game results and progress (stars, achievements, game outcomes and gameplay statistics).
  • Leaderboard entries, and the groups and challenges you join or create.

Why

  • To save your progress.
  • To show leaderboards and rankings.
  • To make groups and challenges between friends work.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Data is stored on our server, operated by Supabase. Progress is also kept on your device.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties.
  • Your nickname and results are visible to other players on leaderboards and in groups you belong to.
  • All communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Supabase) may log standard technical data, such as IP addresses, as part of their normal operation.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • We never ask for your real name, email address or phone number.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete all your data from our server by emailing orikenen.b@gmail.com. Please include your in-game nickname.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13.

Purchases

In-app purchases, if any, are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details.

Changes

We may update this policy. If we do, the date above will change.
