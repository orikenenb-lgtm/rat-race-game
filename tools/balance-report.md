# דוח איזון — מירוץ העכברים v3 ("החיים האמיתיים")

Generated by `node tools/sim.mjs 300 <world> --quiet --md …` (300 games per character × policy, one process per world, ≈7 min). Seeds are fixed, so the report is reproducible.

Policies: **random** (uniform clicks), **cautious** (never borrows to buy, buys only positive cashflow it can pay for in cash, repays loans first, always fixes/hedges/insures, tiny angel checks), **greedy** (careless: buys any positive cashflow even with expensive loans, tiny buffer, always variable rate, never hedges, gives only at the end), **smart** (good heuristic player — the reference for stars: ROI vs loan cost, buy-low/sell-high, sells on great offers, repays expensive debt, locks low rates, upgrades/trims, hedges when the dollar is favourable, small diversified startup checks, gives on a schedule), **tapper** (a kid who always taps the highlighted `primary` button and never touches the loan buttons), **reckless** (buys everything incl. negative cashflow, max leverage, installments/subscriptions, never hedges).

"Months" = the month counter at the moment of escape (as in v1). Games run in free mode up to 1.6× the month limit; "win in time" counts escapes within maxMonths. Stars: ★ = escaped within maxMonths, ★★ ≤ T2, ★★★ ≤ T3 **and final happiness ≥ 60 and an emergency fund ≥ 3 months of expenses** (T3 calibrated so ★★★ ≈ 12% of smart games). The goal must hold on 2 consecutive paydays. v3: two dice on a 40-tile board (≈1.4 rolls per month), Israeli payslip, inflation, index fund, life events, monthly plans; policies choose plans (smart: study early, rest when tired/unhappy, a little overtime when fresh; tapper: the highlighted plan) and life dilemmas. *T2/T3 in the overview are world defaults; the configured per-character thresholds (used by the engine) are listed under each world.
